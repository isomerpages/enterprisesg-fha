			<h4 style="text-transform: uppercase; color: black;"><b>Mottainai Food Tech Pte Ltd</b></h4>
			<p>Mottainai Food Tech Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based food technology company developing sustainable food ingredients by upcycling nutrient-rich food side streams through solid-state fermentation (SSF). The company also provides fermentation and food side stream valorisation expertise to industry partners, supporting product development, process optimisation, and fermentation-based manufacturing. Its innovation pipeline includes Jiro Meat, a plant based minced meat made from upcycled okara.</p>
